Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. About this app

Har Pal Free TV is a free app for watching free-to-air live TV channels on mobile and Smart TV.

2. Your information

This app does not collect, store or share your personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

3. Stored on your device only

Favourites, recently watched channels and settings are saved only on your own device and never leave it.

4. Channels

All channels in this app are free-to-air channels. The app does not host any channel or content itself.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
